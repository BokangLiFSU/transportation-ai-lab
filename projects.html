A traffic-control treatment may improve one outcome while leaving another unchanged. This research evaluates safety separately by crash type and severity, rather than inferring safety benefits from improved traffic operation.

Bridging the gap in rural active transportation

For residents of small rural settlements, a nearby destination may remain difficult to reach on foot or by bicycle. Highways, missing facilities and discontinuous connections can separate residential clusters from essential activities. This project investigates where those barriers occur and which improvements respond to residents’ needs.

Research components. Spatial assessment · Community needs · Prioritization

Study setting
Nine counties in western Washington
Principal investigators

Privacy-preserving transportation data sharing

Detailed transportation records can reveal individual movements even when their value lies in patterns across many travelers. This collaboration investigates how institutions can share and analyze such records while protecting privacy and preserving their usefulness for transportation research.

AI-assisted speed-management infrastructure inventory

Speed-management treatments are difficult to compare when records do not consistently identify their type, location and roadway setting. This research develops an inventory framework for rural corridors and transitions into towns in Washington. The inventory is intended to support corridor screening and the selection of sites for subsequent evaluation.

Research components. Source review · Feature extraction · Spatial inventory

Study setting
Washington rural corridors and rural-to-town transitions

Dr. Angela Kitali; Dr. Sagar Keshari; Dr. Bokang Li

Computer vision for active-transportation facility inventories

Incomplete facility records make it difficult to assess the continuity of rural walking and cycling networks. Missing or inconsistent information can conceal links between local facilities and major roads. This research investigates how imagery-derived inventories can support the identification of facilities and gaps in network coverage.

Research components. Image interpretation · Spatial validation · Network use

Research setting
Pedestrian and bicycle infrastructure

Dr. Angela Kitali; Dr. Thobias Sando; Soud Mbegele; Elia Magesa Machuche; Dr. Bokang Li
