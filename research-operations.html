Transportation data, AI and emerging mobility

How sensing, learning and operational models support the evaluation of electric, automated and shared mobility systems.

Methods

Data science and AISystems and operational analysisScenario evaluation

Research agenda

Transportation AI is a means of investigating transportation conditions, behavior and system performance. Our work uses infrastructure observations, crash narratives, vehicle trajectories and operational records. The analytical task determines the representation: a mapped facility, a collision classification and an incident-duration estimate require different reference evidence and error measures.

Earlier incident-clearance research uses mixture-density networks to represent variation in duration; BRT studies examine travel-time differences and variability. These findings motivate forecasts of delay probabilities as well as expected operating performance. Infrastructure and narrative analysis raise a complementary question: how can an analyst establish which observation supports an inferred condition? Our developing interests concern the reliability of these outputs across locations, their effects on engineering analysis, and the points at which additional evidence or professional review is necessary.

Methods

Computer vision, GIS, statistical learning, traffic-system performance analysis and privacy-preserving data collaboration.
