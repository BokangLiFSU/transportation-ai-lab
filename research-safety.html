Human Behavior and Proactive Safety

How road design, traffic controls and emerging technologies change behavior, exposure and the risk of serious injury.

Methods

Statistical inferenceBehavior and risk analysisSafety evaluation

We investigate the conditions under which traffic incidents produce additional crashes and how response strategies affect their consequences. Dr. Angela Kitali’s Florida research combines incident records, traffic measurements, roadway geometry and rainfall to identify secondary crashes and estimate their likelihood. Related Bayesian before-and-after studies evaluate pedestrian countdown signals, adaptive signal control and transit signal priority.

The AI agenda builds on two unresolved problems: defining risk as queues evolve and transferring predictions between corridors with different operating conditions. We are interested in models that incorporate changing traffic states, crash narratives and infrastructure attributes while producing reliable probabilities for rare events. A separate line examines how these estimates can inform treatment evaluation without confusing predictive associations with intervention effects.
