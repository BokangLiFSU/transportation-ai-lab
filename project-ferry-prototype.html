Research context

Small passenger ferries require navigation and docking capabilities suited to coastal conditions. This campus-collaborator study investigates these capabilities through early prototyping.

Approach

The study combines a small prototype with docking-target detection and a two-dimensional, waypoint-based navigation model. Simulation examines vessel guidance under environmental conditions.

The Duck-E prototype uses readily available components for preliminary navigation and docking-target detection. The conference abstract reports target identification within 5% error over distances of 1–2.35 metres. A two-dimensional waypoint model incorporates weather information for route simulation. Automated docking, hydrodynamic refinement and richer current and vessel-traffic information remain development tasks.
