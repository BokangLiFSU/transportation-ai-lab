  • A Bayesian quantile-based analysis of micromobility usage and challenges
    2026 · Journal article · Cities

Research question

Shared mobility can be available across a city while actual use remains uneven. This study examines how neighborhood characteristics relate to e-bike and e-scooter trip rates in Tacoma, Washington.

Data and methods

The analysis uses Tacoma pilot data from May 2021 to August 2023 at census-block-group level. Bayesian quantile regression examines how associations vary across low, medium and high levels of use, rather than estimating a single average relationship.

The study considers socioeconomic characteristics together with the built and natural environment. Its distributional approach helps identify relationships that would be obscured by an analysis focused only on average ridership. The observational design concerns patterns of use within the pilot setting.

Research contribution

  • The study analyzes Razor e-bike and e-scooter records from May 2021 to August 2023 at census-block-group level.
  • Bayesian quantile regression estimates neighborhood associations at different trip-rate levels, rather than fitting a single conditional-mean relationship.
  • The analysis describes observed use; estimating unmet demand or a service-policy effect would require additional evidence.
