Working papers

Public manuscripts listed separately from peer-reviewed journal articles.

Fragmentation in U.S. Micromobility Governance: A Multi-Level Analysis of State Statutes and Local Ordinances

The working paper compares state-level statutes and local ordinances governing e-bikes and e-scooters. Gower dissimilarity and a Policy Fragmentation Index describe differences in vehicle classification, operating rules, safety provisions and licensing. Authors: Angela Mashalla, Abdul Shaban Ngereza, Thobias Sando, Angela Kitali, Emmanuel Kidando and Sagar Keshari.

Working paper on SSRN · DOI

Not Just a Checkbox: Policy Mechanisms Shape Inclusive Shared Micromobility Systems

The working paper codes 381 documents from 235 U.S. programs, identifying 33 mechanisms across nine policy areas. Its comparison examines explicit pricing provisions, adaptive vehicle types and distribution locations. Greater policy specificity is associated with the high-performing comparison group; the design does not establish that those provisions caused the observed performance.

Working paper on SSRN · DOI

Connecting Bicyclists and Transit: A Multimodal Routing Tool with Bicycle Facilities Scoring

The platform combines ArcGIS Network Analyst, General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) feeds and transit-routing services. Direct cycling, bike–bus–bike and transit-only alternatives can be compared through journey summaries and segment-level bicycle-facility scores. Authors: Raphael Mrema, Angela Kitali, Thobias Sando, Panick Kalambay and Monica Deibel.
