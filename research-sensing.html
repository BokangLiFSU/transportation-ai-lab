Research Focus

Crash narratives, roadway images and geometric observations describe different aspects of transportation infrastructure and operations. Our published work uses natural language processing to identify wrong-way-driving records and mixture-density networks to model incident-clearance duration. Current infrastructure research examines the detection and mapping of sidewalks, bicycle facilities and speed-management treatments from imagery and LiDAR.

Research Questions

We are interested in reconciling these observations when they disagree, omit essential attributes or cover different locations and periods. Research questions concern spatial correspondence, uncertainty in inferred features and updates after infrastructure changes. Evaluation must consider the information recovered, errors in the resulting inventory, and their effects on subsequent transportation analysis. Pedestrian routes raise further measurement questions about usable width, clearance and localized obstructions that a presence-or-absence inventory cannot resolve.
