Research focus

Transportation records contain information that is difficult to recover through conventional databases alone. Our published work classifies wrong-way-driving crashes from police narratives and uses mixture-density networks to predict incident-clearance duration. Infrastructure research extends to detecting and mapping sidewalks, bicycle facilities and speed-management treatments from imagery and light detection and ranging (LiDAR) data.

Research questions

Our longer-term interests include learning across text, imagery and geometric observations, particularly where data are incomplete or inconsistently recorded. Specific questions concern how to align observations of the same facility, distinguish a missing feature from missing coverage, and update an inventory when road conditions change. We also study how privacy protections affect the transportation analyses that collaborating institutions can perform.
