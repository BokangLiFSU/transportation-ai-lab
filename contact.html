Research Collaboration

We welcome enquiries on transportation systems and artificial intelligence, including opportunities to connect engineering, behavioral and computational research. A useful starting point is the research question, the transportation setting and the evidence or decisions involved.

Student Research

Students interested in research should email Dr. Angela Kitali with their background, relevant experience and a short statement of research interests. Projects may involve statistical analysis, GIS, computer vision, transportation observations or policy analysis. Research opportunities depend on active work and supervision capacity.
